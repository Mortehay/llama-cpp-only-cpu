## The live thread: b943998

One theme - **the sheet path could not see a moderate close-up**. Four parts:

1. **`src/sprite_generator/framing.py` (new)** - a CLIP bottom-band detector
   for "are the feet in frame". Semantic on purpose, because geometry cannot
   answer it: the angles LoRA frames a torso and a whole body to the *same*
   478-512 of 512 box, and `figure_ratio` is **non-monotonic** in the defect
   (cropped cells 0.980-1.726 against complete ones 0.900-1.176). Six
   geometric detectors were measured and all six failed for that one reason.
   - Scoring the whole cell measured 67.6% against a 65% base rate - useless,
     because CLIP resizes to 224 and boots at the foot of a 512 canvas become
     a handful of pixels. The bottom 30% at 2x is what works. **The view was
     the defect, not the model.**
   - Worth, measured over 68 hand-labelled cells across TWO characters:
     **12 of 13 cropped caught, 0 of 55 complete wrongly flagged**, at a fixed
     cut near 0.49.
   - **Not clean, and the limit is structural.** One cropped cell scores 0.722
     while a complete one scores 0.487, so no threshold separates every pair;
     the operating point sits on that boundary. 0.49 trades one wasted redraw
     per boundary case for catching the crop.
   - **Per-sheet calibration was measured and does NOT help** - the
     turnaround references score 0.9931 and 0.9932 on the two subjects, so
     there is no offset to correct. Do not rebuild that idea.
   - 1.18 s/cell on CPU, parked in host RAM, never placed on the card: it runs
     inside the denoise subprocess, which already holds the transformer.

2. **`sheet_qc.py`** - `framing` joins `CHECKS`, `feet_in_frame` lands in
   `measure()`, `failures()` flags it, and `score()` gains a term weighted
   2.0. The score term is the non-obvious part: **a redraw keeps the
   best-SCORING attempt**, so omitting it would let a round replace a
   correctly framed cell with a close-up that scores better on colour - and
   the failure list would then flag the cell the loop just chose.

3. **`pixelate.py`** - the PINNED-scale path now warns when a sprite exceeds
   the cell, instead of letting `Image.paste` clip it silently on a negative
   offset. This is the latent bug the tripwires record (measured 0 of 168
   clipped on `06122e74`). Still a warning, not a refusal: the uniform
   sheet-wide scale is deliberate.

4. **`models.toml`** - the CLIP stanza stopped ignoring `model.safetensors`.
   `0.005 -> 1.72 GB`, tier `required`, because `framing.py` needs the vision
   tower while the SD1.5 lane only ever wanted the tokenizer.

## The framing check HAS now been exercised - 2026-10-27, after the above

Not through a build. The check reads decoded pixels off `cut_*.png` and never
touches the card, so it was run over all **210 cells of `_job_2ff8af36`** (the
Q2_K baseline) in a throwaway `docker exec -e FRAMING_CHECK=1`. CPU only, no
job queued, nothing in either container changed. **229.5 s total, 1.09 s/cell**
- the docstring's 1.18 s/cell holds.

```
feet_in_frame : min 0.0126  p10 0.5282  median 0.9837  p90 0.9992  max 0.9998
below 0.49    : 19 of 210 (9.0%)
recorded close_up : 3     overlap with framing: 2
```

**It sees what nothing else sees.** Of the 19 cells it flags:

- **18 have `figure_ratio` below 1.85**, i.e. outside the close-up band the
  contract calibrated (close-ups 1.85-1.98) - geometry saw nothing.
- **13 had NO check fire at all.** They are invisible to the entire existing
  QC suite.

Four were inspected by eye, and the check was right in all four:

| cell | `feet_in_frame` | `figure_ratio` | recorded | what it actually is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `idle\|s\|0` | high | 1.0322 | clean | full body, boots on the ground - control |
| `cast\|s\|2` | 0.0190 | 1.3517 | **clean** | **cut off at mid-thigh, no feet** |
| `cast\|se\|4` | 0.1023 | **1.0073** | **clean** | **cut off at the thigh, no feet** |
| `damage\|ne\|1` | 0.9926 | - | **`close_up`** | boots fully visible; figure merely hunched |

`cast|se|4` is the one to keep. Its `figure_ratio` of **1.0073 is better than
the contract's "clean" band of 1.07-1.09**, and the figure is visibly cut off
at the thigh. That is the non-monotonicity argument confirmed on a cell the
detector was not calibrated against, rather than inferred.

`damage|ne|1` is the mirror case: `close_up` fired, framing passed it, and
framing was right - a crouched pose compresses opaque area against a standing
reference. So the 19-against-3 gap is not simply framing being trigger-happy;
on the one cell where the two checks disagreed, **the old check was the false
positive**.

**Flags cluster hard by action:** `cast` 10/30 (33%), `use` 4/30 (13%),
`attack` and `sway` 2/30, `idle` 1/30, `walk` and `damage` **0/30**. The two

## The gap to close first

`FRAMING_CHECK`, `FRAMING_MIN`, `FRAMING_BAND` and `FRAMING_ZOOM` are read by
`framing.py` and appear **nowhere** in `compose/develop/.env.example`, and are
unset in both containers. The check therefore ships off and undiscoverable.

This is one letter away from the `EDIT_ENABLE` / `EDIT_ENABLED` trap, and it
fails the same way:

- it must be set on **both** `sprite-generator` and `sprite-worker`;
- the containers must be **recreated**, not restarted, for compose to re-read
  the environment;
- and with the flag off, `feet_in_frame` is simply absent - which reads as
  "every cell is framed correctly" rather than "nothing was judged", exactly
  the distinction `facing` draws when its reference is too weak.

## What cannot travel, and what that costs

- **`compose/develop/.env`** - secrets. Recreate by hand.
- **`images/` (~4.3 GB)** - the per-job work dirs. A sheet is redrawable
  **only** while `images/_job_<id>/` survives; without it
  `POST /api/jobs/{id}/redraw` answers 410. **Existing sheets will not be
  redrawable on the laptop.** 71% of that bulk is `embeds_*.pt`, which nothing
  reads once a build has finished (`make prune-job-cache`).
- **The ~149 GB under the distro's `/models`** - re-fetch with `make provision`.
- **Postgres (`stats_db` volume)** - job history and the training-reference
  verdict columns stay on this box.
- **Every measurement in `.ai/` was taken on a 12 GB card.** If the laptop's
  VRAM differs, these do not carry and must be re-measured before being
  quoted: Q2_K resident at ~30.1 s/cell; the Q6_K group-offload figures
  (10.7 GiB free after placement, 68.8 s/cell all-in); the brain's
  `--n-cpu-moe 24` split at ~8.3 GB VRAM; and the VAE tiling thresholds.
  Host RAM matters as much - group offload streams weights from it, and this
  box has 41 GB inside WSL.
